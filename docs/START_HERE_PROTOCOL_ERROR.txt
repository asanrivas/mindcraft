📝 WHAT WENT WRONG

Your bot is trying to communicate with your Minecraft server.
But they're "speaking different languages" (protocol versions).

Example:
  • Bot speaking: Minecraft 1.20.1 protocol
  • Server speaking: Minecraft 1.20.4 protocol
  • Result: 💥 PartialReadError

Fix: Make them speak the same language!
